About Me

Hello! I'm Avi Bansal, a senior studying Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University. I'm passionate about creating technology that works for everyone, regardless of their abilities.

Through my coursework in Human-Computer Interaction and Accessible Computing, I've developed a deep appreciation for inclusive design principles and their impact on users' lives.

Accessibility Interests

My primary focus areas in accessible computing include:

  • Mobile accessibility - ensuring mobile applications and responsive websites are usable by people with disabilities
  • Screen reader compatibility - designing interfaces that work seamlessly with assistive technologies

Why Accessible Computing?

I am interested in this field because of how accessibility principles can improve inclusivity in product design and software development. My previous coursework in Human-Computer Interaction opened my eyes to the importance of considering diverse user needs from the very beginning of the design process. I believe that accessible technology is not just a legal requirement but a fundamental aspect of good engineering.

Collaboration Opportunities

I'm eager to collaborate on team projects that focus on:

  • Developing accessible mobile applications
  • Testing and improving screen reader compatibility in web interfaces
  • Conducting usability studies with users who have disabilities
  • Creating inclusive design guidelines for software development teams

If you share similar interests or have complementary skills, I'd love to connect!
